Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel or reschedule a camp?

Yes. You can cancel or reschedule up to 14 days before the camp start date for a full refund. Between 7-14 days, a 50% fee applies. Less than 7 days, no refund but you can transfer to another session.

How do I track my child's progress?

Visit the Skill Progress tab in your dashboard. You'll see progress bars for each skill module, plus direct feedback from instructors after each session.

When are certificates issued?

Certificates are issued within 5 days of the final showcase and appear in your Certificates tab. You can download PDFs anytime.

What if my child has special needs?

We welcome all children. Please email us at parents@leadcamp.com at least 2 weeks before camp starts so we can prepare accommodations and support.
