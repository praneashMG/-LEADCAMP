Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions we hear from parents and campers. Can't find what you're looking for? Just send us a message.

What ages do you accept?

We welcome campers aged 11–17 across our three program levels. Beginner (ages 11–13), Intermediate (ages 13–15), and Advanced (ages 15–17). Every camper is placed in a group with peers of similar age and experience.

My child is very shy. Will they be comfortable?

Absolutely. Shy campers are our specialty. We never force anyone to speak before they're ready. On day one, we start with small pair exercises and gradually build up. By day five, every single camper performs—but at their own pace, with our full support.

What should my child bring to camp?

Just a water bottle, a notebook, and a willingness to try. We provide all camp materials, workbooks, lunch, and snacks. Campers should wear comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes (no sandals during activities).

Do you offer refunds or payment plans?

Yes. We offer full refunds up to 14 days before the session begins, and 50% refunds up to 7 days before. We also offer flexible payment plans on all programs and seasonal packages. Contact us for details.

Can parents watch the showcase?

Yes—in fact, we strongly encourage it! The final showcase is a celebration, and family members are welcome. We'll send you the exact time and location one week in advance. We also provide professional photos and video afterward.

Are coaches background-checked?

Every single coach, mentor, and volunteer undergoes a full background check, reference verification, and youth protection training before working with campers. Safety is our number one priority, always.
