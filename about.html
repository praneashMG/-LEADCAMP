Our Story

From A Small Workshop To A Movement

What started as a single weekend workshop has grown into one of the most trusted youth public speaking programs in the region.

2019

The year our first camp welcomed 18 campers

Est.

2019

LEADCAMP began when our founder—a former competitive speaker and educator—noticed something troubling: talented young people were staying silent because they'd never been taught how to speak up. Not just how to project their voice, but how to believe in what they had to say.

The first camp welcomed 18 campers in a rented community hall. By the end of the week, every single one of them stood on a makeshift stage and delivered a speech. Some cried. Some laughed. All of them grew. That week, we knew we'd found something special.

Today, we've trained over 500 young speakers across 15+ sessions. Our alumni have gone on to win debate competitions, lead student councils, deliver TEDx talks, and—most importantly— carry themselves with the quiet confidence that comes from knowing their voice matters.

What Drives Us

Mission, Vision & Values

Every decision we make—from curriculum design to camper ratios—is guided by a clear set of principles that put young people first.

Our Mission

To equip every young person with the skills, confidence, and mindset to speak up, stand out, and lead with purpose—regardless of their starting point, background, or natural ability. We believe public speaking isn't a talent you're born with; it's a skill you build.

Our Vision

A generation of young leaders who don't just speak—they communicate with empathy, conviction, and clarity. We see a world where every classroom, boardroom, and community has voices that were once too afraid to be heard, now leading the conversation.

Our Core Values

Six principles that shape everything we do.

Courage First

We celebrate the bravery it takes to speak, not just the polish of the final speech.

Radical Encouragement

Every camper gets specific, genuine praise. Growth starts with feeling seen.

Community Over Competition

Campers lift each other up. There are no winners here—only people growing together.

Practice Over Theory

Campers speak every single day. Skills are built through reps, not lectures.

Safety & Belonging

Camp is a space where mistakes are safe and every identity is welcome and respected.

Long-Term Growth

We measure success by what campers do months later—not just on showcase day.

What Makes Us Different

We Don't Just Teach Speaking. We Build People.

Many programs focus on technique. We start with the person. Before a camper learns to project their voice, they learn to trust it. Before they learn persuasive structure, they discover what they actually believe.

  • Small Groups, Real Attention

    Maximum 14 campers per group means every voice gets heard—literally.

  • Daily Practice, Not Theory

    Campers speak every single day. Confidence comes from reps, not lectures.

  • Real Stage, Real Applause

    The week ends with a genuine showcase on a real stage—lights, mic, and all.
